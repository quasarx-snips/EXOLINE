# Moon selection

The Phase 1 moon roster is gameplay-oriented, not exhaustive. It covers the major/strategic moons most useful to EXOLINE's resource and landing loops across Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune and Pluto.

Selected: Moon, Phobos, Deimos, Io, Europa, Ganymede, Callisto, Titan, Enceladus, Rhea, Iapetus, Dione, Tethys, Mimas, Titania, Oberon, Ariel, Umbriel, Miranda, Triton, Charon.

The selection intentionally favors bodies that can add meaningful combinations of water/ice, metals, silicates, volatiles, rare minerals and organics within the existing EXOLINE resource economy.
